About the property

Description property

Situated within the established and popular residential development of The Meadows, No. 22 is a well-proportioned four-bedroom semi-detached residence extending to approximately 110 m², offering an excellent combination of generous accommodation, private outdoor space and convenient village living.

The property is presented in good condition throughout and provides a practical and versatile layout ideally suited to modern family life. The accommodation briefly comprises an entrance hallway with guest WC, a comfortable sitting room and a well-proportioned kitchen/dining room, while upstairs there are four bedrooms, including three double bedrooms and a single bedroom, together with the family bathroom.

One of the property's notable features is the generous enclosed rear garden, predominantly laid in lawn and providing an excellent private outdoor space for families, children or entertaining during the summer months. To the front, an extended driveway provides off-street parking, while the rear garden also benefits from a timber garden shed providing useful additional storage.

The property enjoys an exceptionally convenient position within Smithborough, with the village centre and a range of local amenities within easy reach. Smithborough provides a strong community environment with local retail facilities, a pub, takeaway and a range of sporting and community amenities, while primary education and childcare facilities are also available locally.

For commuters, the property's position on the N54 provides excellent connectivity to both Monaghan and Cavan, while Clones is also readily accessible. This combination of village convenience and regional connectivity makes Smithborough an increasingly attractive location for those seeking a more relaxed setting without sacrificing accessibility to larger towns and employment centres.

With its generous accommodation, four-bedroom layout, substantial rear garden and excellent location within an established residential development, No. 22 The Meadows represents an excellent opportunity for first-time buyers, families and those seeking a well-connected home within a welcoming village environment.
